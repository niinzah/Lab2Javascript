What are my skills?

Here are some of the skills I have acquired:

Adaptability

Having moved countries several times, starting when I was 9 years old has made me very adaptable. I often seek new environments and situations as I feel comfortable in them. This has come in useful for me when I moved for my studies and work and has allowed me to meet various kinds of people.

Empathy

Something I had since young, as I felt a lot of connection to animals. This skill has been very useful when I worked as a User Researcher. By being able to "put myself in the other person's shoes" I was able to provide useful guidance in how to improve the app to fulfill the users' needs.

Curiosity

Hand-in-hand with being empathetic, being curious has always been in my core. I often would ask the question "why?" since young and I was nicknamed "Nina Nyfiken". This has also helped me be a successful User Researcher as I often wanted to understand the root of why people would behave a certain way.
