What experiences do I have?

Here is a timeline of my experiences.

Timeline

  1. Moved abroad

    2008

    When I was only 9 years old, my family and I moved from Sweden to China. I started going to an English-speaking school and had no option but to adapt and learn. This is when I fully was outside of my comfort zone and had to learn how to adapt.

  2. Finished School

    2016

    The final years of school was when I fully learnt how to be disciplined and focused. I was not going as well as I wanted to at the beginning, using the disappointment I was motivated to be the most focused version of myself. Which allowed me to achieve good grades. I was then set with the challenge of moving abroad again, to the UK for my bachelors.

  3. Bachelor Degree

    2020

    Using the same focus from my high school, I was able to achieve a Bachelor's Degree in Engineeering. Other challenges came along the way, such as COVID lockdown in my final semester. Using my adaptability, I was able to adjust my way of working, creating a comfortable study-set up in my room rather than the library, I was able to achieve the results I wanted.
